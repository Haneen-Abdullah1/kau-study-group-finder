The idea

The problem

Many students find it hard to search for people who really want to study the same courses.

Our solution

KAU Study Group Finder: each student chooses a course they find hard to study alone and joins other students studying the same course.

Benefits

  • You will know other people who take the same course
  • You can share notes and explain hard points to each other
  • You can keep each other motivated
  • You can find a study group easily instead of searching for days

How it works

  1. Create an account with your KAU email
  2. Log in to see which group you want to join
  3. Choose the course you need help with
  4. Join the study group and start studying together
